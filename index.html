My Process

From Understanding the Problem to Building the Experience

A flexible process that adapts to the product, its users, and the scope of the work. Each stage has a clear purpose, keeping decisions connected from the first requirement to the final experience.

Discover

Understand the brief, audience, business context, and problem. Review available research, competitors, and existing experiences before deciding what needs to change.

01
02

Strategize

Turn findings into a practical direction. Define the product goals, key features, user journeys, priorities, and scope of the experience.

Design

Translate the direction into user flows, wireframes, interface systems, and interactive prototypes. Refine the experience through iteration and usability thinking.

03
04

Build

Prepare the design for implementation and work through the build with an understanding of front-end technologies. Use AI-assisted creative coding where it supports the project, without overstating engineering depth.

Launch & Improve

Review the finished experience, address friction, and identify opportunities for improvement. A product should keep evolving as its users and needs become clearer.
